    How to play

    Goal

    Be the first to land on tile 100. Everyone starts on tile 1 and takes turns rolling one die.

    Your turn

    • Press Roll (or Space / R). Your piece hops that many tiles, following the numbers in a zig-zag.
    • ▲ Ladders — if you stop on the bottom of a ladder, you climb to the top.
    • ▼ Snakes — if you stop on a snake's head, you slide down to its tail. It's just luck; the match goes on!
    • Small ▲ / ▼ labels in the corner of a tile tell you where it leads.
    • Pieces can share a tile — nobody gets bumped.

    Finishing

    • Exact finish (default): you must roll exactly what you need. If your roll is too high, you stay put.
    • Bounce back (optional): if your roll is too high, you move to 100 and bounce back the extra steps.
    • Bonus on six (optional): rolling a 6 earns another roll, up to two bonus rolls in a row.

    Fair play

    All dice — yours and the computer's — come from the same secure random source. Computer players never get lucky rolls, hidden boosts or different rules; they only pace themselves. Each match summary shows the real roll distribution.

    Modes & worlds

    • Classic — the standard board at a relaxed pace.
    • Quick Roll — the same rules with faster animations.
    • Wild Board — a freshly generated, balanced board. Enter a seed to replay a board.
    • New worlds unlock as you play. Worlds change the look and sound only — never the rules.

    Controls

    Space/R roll · Esc menu · M mute · or just tap the buttons. Everything works with keyboard, mouse and touch.
